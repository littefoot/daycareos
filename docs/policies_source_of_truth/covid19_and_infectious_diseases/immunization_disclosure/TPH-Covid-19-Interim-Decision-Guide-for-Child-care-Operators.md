COVID-19 INTERIM DECISION GUIDE FOR CHILD CARE OPERATORS  
TO DIRECT COHORTS TO SELF-ISOLATE
Updated September 17th, 2021
This tool is to be used by Child Care Operators to assist them in making a decision to dismiss a case’s cohort(s) for self-isolation while awaiting  
the results of the Toronto Public Health (TPH) Investigation. TPH will investigate all positive cases related to a child care and notify their close  
contacts in a child care through a letter. The Communicable Disease Investigator (CDI) will contact the Child Care Operator and advise of next 
steps. If the Child Care Operator has any questions while they await for the CDI to contact them, they can contact the TPH COVID-19 Hotline by 
email at publichealth@toronto.ca  or by calling 416-338-7600.

*Please Note:
• Child Care Operators will not ask other staff or children potentially exposed to the positive case outside of the cohort(s) to   
 self-isolate. They will await direction from the TPH CDI. If an individual is symptomatic or a close contact of a positive case, then  
 follow the child care settings protocols and the COVID-19 Decision Tool for Child Care Attendees and Staff.
• If the individual with COVID-19 interacts with multiple cohorts or visits many child care settings/cohort(s), the Child Care  
 Operator will flag this for TPH and await the direction of the TPH CDI.
• The Child Care Operator should maintain a list of COVID-19 vaccination information of staff/child. This should be voluntarily  
 disclosed by the child’s parent/guardian. Collection of staff vaccination information should follow child care policy. This list is  
 confidential and the Child Care Operator or designate should only use this information as required to dismiss children/staff that  
 are part of affected cohorts who are not fully vaccinated with a COVID-19 vaccine*. Children/staff who are fully vaccinated with a  
 COVID-19 vaccine and do not have a symptom(s) of COVID-19, do not need to be dismissed unless directed otherwise by   
 TPH. TPH will complete an investigation of the case and their cohorts. Return to child care of individuals may be permitted at the  
 discretion of the TPH CDI based on assessment of the investigation. Individuals who were previously positive for COVID-19 in the  
 last 90 days or less and have been cleared by public health may be permitted to return to child care. TPH will provide this  
 direction to these previously positive for COVID-19 close contacts. 
• *Fully vaccinated is defined as an individual ≥14 days after receiving their second dose of a two-dose COVID-19 vaccine series  
 or a single dose of a one-dose COVID-19 vaccine series.
Dismiss members of case’s cohort(s) for self-isolation who have not been fully vaccinated with a COVID-19 vaccine*, until the Child Care 
Operator receives further direction from the TPH CDI. The case’s siblings and household members who are not fully vaccinated* with a 
COVID-19 vaccine, also need to stay home.
