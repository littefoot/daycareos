COVID-19 Decision Tool for Child Care Staff and Visitors
For all situations listed in the flow chart below: 
• (A)  Close Contact
• Anyone who is a close contact of a positive case will have to self-isolate for 10 days, and should arrange to get tested. 
• If someone is self-isolating in the home, household members can go to child care/day camp/school/work but should not leave the home for other non-essential 
reasons. Essential reasons also include errands such as obtaining groceries, attending medical appointments or picking up prescriptions. Household members who 
are fully vaccinated with a COVID-19 vaccine or have tested positive for COVID-19 in the last 90 days and have since been cleared are not required to stay home.
• If the close contact is: (i) without symptoms and fully vaccinated (it has been 14 days or more after getting a second dose of a two dose COVID-19 vaccine series or a 
single dose of a one dose vaccine series) or (ii) is without symptoms and tested positive for COVID-19 in the last 90 day and was cleared by public health:
  - They may not be required to self-isolate, but they will need to monitor for any COVID-19 symptoms for 10 days.
  - They should still go for testing if recommended.  
  - They are recommended to avoid non-essential visits to settings with vulnerable populations or where there is a large number of unvaccinated people.  
 They may continue attending these settings for essential reasons (e.g. work, school, child care).  
• (B) Travel Outside of Canada
• Anyone who has travelled outside of Canada is required to self-isolate for 14 days. They are to follow the federal requirements for travel.
• If the traveller does not have symptoms, household members can go to child care/day camp/school/work but should not leave the home for other non-essential 
reasons. Essential reasons also include errands such as obtaining groceries, attending medical appointments or picking up prescriptions. Household members who 
are fully vaccinated with a COVID-19 vaccine are not required to stay home.
• There are some exemptions for people eligible to enter Canada who also meet specific federal conditions for fully vaccinated travellers.
• If the traveller does not have symptoms and is exempt from federal quarantine requirements, household members do not need to stay home.
