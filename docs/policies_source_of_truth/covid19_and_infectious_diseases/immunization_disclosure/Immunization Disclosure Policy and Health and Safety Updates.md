Immunization Disclosure Policy 
I am writing today in follow-up to the memo you received on August 18, 2021 related to 
the government’s recent announcement of an immunization disclosure policy for all 
licensed child care settings.  
The Chief Medical Officer of Health will be issuing instructions for licensed child care in 
accordance with the authority granted by the Reopening Ontario (A Flexible Response 
to COVID-19) Act, 2020. These instructions require that all licensees establish, 
implement and report on a COVID-19 Immunization Disclosure Policy. All licensees 
under the Child Care and Early Years Act, 2014 are subject to these requirements until 
such time as they are revoked/rescinded.  
Requirements will come into effect on September 7, 2021 and are anticipated to be in 
force for the 2021/22 school year. Recognizing that licensees will require time to 
implement the changes, dates for compliance will be communicated soon, once 
instructions have been issued. 
Vaccination is one of the most important tools for helping stop the spread of COVID-19 
and is a particularly important tool for adults working with young children who are not 
yet able to receive a vaccine. Promoting vaccination through a consistent provincial 
policy for schools and licensed child care settings will support our children, families and 
communities in the coming months.

Statistical Data Submission 
Licensed child care programs are also required to provide statistical information to the 
ministry related to the vaccine status of staff and home child care providers. Only 
aggregate numbers are to be submitted - no personal health information or identifiable 
information should be provided to the ministry.  
As a licensed child care program, you are subject to these reporting requirements and 
will be required to submit data to the Ministry of Education on a monthly basis. More 
details are provided in the attached resource guide. 
All licensees operating fewer than 25 child care centres, as well as all home child care 
agencies, must report using an online form. One form must be submitted for each 
licence. Licensees operating more than 25 licensed child care centres may choose to 
report using a Ministry of Education reporting template (e.g., Excel spreadsheet) that 
includes all of their licences. If you would like to use this template, please email the 
Licensed Child Care Helpdesk at childcare_ontario@ontario.ca. 
The deadline for your first data submission is September 15, 2021.

Other Health and Safety Updates 
As you are aware, on August 3, 2021 the Operational Guidance for Child Care During 
COVID-19 Outbreak document was updated. These changes are based on advice and 
support by the Chief Medical Officer of Health. To support those updates, amendments 
have been made to the COVID-19 specific rules in the child care licensing regulation, 
O.Reg 137/15 under the Child Care and Early Years Act, 2014.  
The following regulatory changes will take effect shortly: 
• Licensees are no longer required to ensure daily attendance records for child 
care staff that include contact information and time of arrival and departure. 
• Essential volunteers are no longer prohibited in licensed childcare settings 
• Licensees are no longer permitted to implement COVID-19 policies and protocols 
that prohibit parents from entering the child care premises.  
Licensees may continue to offer drop off and pick up procedures where parents do not 
enter the premises; however, if a parent wishes to enter the premises, they must be 
permitted to do so, unless a licensee is implementing a direction of a medical officer of 
health in respect of COVID-19.  
If parents and volunteers do enter the child care premises, licensees must ensure all 
requirements for those who enter the premises are met, including screening for 
symptoms of COVID-19 and maintaining up-to-date daily attendance records that 
include time in and time out. 
Updates to Operational Guidance for Child Care and Early Years Programs 
In light of the current public health environment and based on advice from the Chief 
Medical Officer of Health, masking requirements for child care and early years workers 
have been updated.  
 
Medical masks (surgical/procedural) are required to be worn by child care and early 
years staff/providers outdoors if two metres of distance from others cannot be 
maintained. Eye protection is also required if a staff member/ provider comes within two 
metres of an unmasked individual both indoors and outdoors, as per occupational 
health and safety requirements.  At the advice of the local public health unit, licensees 
and providers may choose to implement additional masking measures based on local 
circumstances.  
 
The ministry is working to update the child care, before and after school programs, 
EarlyON Child and Family Program, and First Nations child care and child and family 
program guidance documents to reflect this change and they will be made available 
shortly. We encourage you to communicate this information as soon as possible to your 
communities, including child care and early years staff and providers as well as 
parents/families.

The government will monitor the COVID-19 situation, including ongoing risks related to 
variants of concern (VOCs), and alignment with broader provincial guidance and 
direction. The ministry will continue to work with the Chief Medical Officer of Health and 
local public health units (PHUs) to assess key measures to inform and update provincial 
guidance and direction, including lifting measures when appropriate.  
 
As always, child care licensees must comply with any direction of their local public 
health unit. Licensees may also choose to implement additional measures based on 
local circumstances in consultation with local public health.
