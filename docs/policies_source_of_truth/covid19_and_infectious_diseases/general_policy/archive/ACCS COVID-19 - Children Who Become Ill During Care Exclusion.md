POLICY 
To ensure the health and well being of children within the early learning and child care setting 
for Emergency Child Care, children who become ill while attending the program will be 
immediately isolated and required to leave the program. Staff must consistently assess the 
health of children in care. 
PROCEDURES 
When to exclude 
Child care employees should exclude a sick child when the child has any signs and/or 
symptoms of ill health or if the child is unable to participate in regular programming because of 
illness. 
Any child who develops symptoms of ill health including symptoms of ill health related to 
COVID-19 must leave the child care program.   
Symptoms include 
• Feeling feverish
• Coughing
• Runny nose
• Nasal congestion
• Difficulty breathing
• Vomiting
• Diarrhea
• Sore throat
• Hoarseness/difficulty swallowing
• Myalgia (body ache)
If the child has any symptoms (including fever, cough, difficulty breathing, vomiting, or diarrhea), 
the child must be isolated immediately from the other children in a separate room and 
supervised at all times. 
The parent must be notified to arrange for pick-up of the child. If the parent is not reached an 
emergency contact person will be contacted to pick up the child.  
Every effort must be made to keep the child comfortable until someone arrives to take him or 
her home. 
Staff must document the situation in the Daily Communication Log and notify the Supervisor or 
designate immediately to ensure that this information is managed and recorded in the child’s file 
on the Ill Health/Potential Allegation Record and report the incident to the Health Department, 
as necessary/appropriate.

How to exclude 
• Supervise the child in a designated room with a hand washing sink or hand sanitizer 
available  
• Notify parents/caregivers of the sick child for pick up 
• Only one staff should be in the designated room and attempt physical distancing. If 
physical distancing cannot be avoided, staff should wear a mask and gloves. In addition, 
staff should perform hand hygiene and attempt to not touch their face with unwashed 
hands 
• Increase ventilation in the designated exclusion room if possible (e.g., open windows)  
• Clean and disinfect the area immediately after the child has been sent home 
• The Health Department recommendations for staff and children who were in the same 
room with the ill child will be communicated to all staff and parent/caregivers and children 
in the group.   
• Staff in the facility should self-monitor for symptoms for the next 14 days.  
• If the symptomatic child tests negative for COVID-19 or is symptom free for 48 hrs and 
receives approval to return by a physician or Durham Health Department then all staff, 
parents/caregivers will be notified.    
Returning from exclusion due to illness 
• Staff/children who are being managed by Durham Public Health (e.g., confirmed cases
of COVID-19, household contacts of cases) should follow instructions from Durham
Health Department to determine when to return to the facility. Confirmation from Durham
Health Department will be required.
• Child cannot return to program until symptom free for 48 hrs and clear by a physician or
Durham Health Department.
