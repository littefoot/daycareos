All Child Care Solutions Inc.

SANITARY PRACTICE POLICY

* All Home Providers, Home Visitors and Persons Ordinarily on the Premises, as well as children; must follow the municipality’s public health recommendations for proper hand washing (public health signs are posted wherever designated hand washing stations are)*

Children physical Facility:

Office, coatroom, kitchen, toilets

Sink washroom floors are swept, washed, sanitized each day. They are cleaned after the children had lunch. Carpet, rooms are cleaned as required. (Anywhere a child resides during their day at a home child care site.)

Washrooms:

Staff/home provider is responsible for maintaining an adequate supply of paper towels, soap, toilet paper. Also available bleach bucket, broom, and clean floor mop.

Kitchen:

The floor is swept and washed daily with disinfectant (Bleach Solution). The counters and sinks are cleaned daily with disinfectant (Following disinfectant recommendations from public health). Stove, fridges and cupboards are cleaned daily or as required. An industrial dishwasher or an approved “three sink method” with bleach is used to clean all dishes and utensils.

Hats or hairnets are to be worn by kitchen staff if required. Kitchen is out of bounds to everyone except the cook while food is being prepared. The cook or anyone preparing or serving food is to practice proper hand-washing methods using germicidal soap and hot water, washing for approximately 30 seconds in order to ensure proper cleaning of hands. They are also to wear an apron or smock if necessary. (Cook must wear appropriate clothing) children are not allowed in the kitchen at any time.

Toys and Equipment:

Toys and play equipment are disinfected weekly or sooner if they become soiled. Shelves and children’s cubbies are disinfected weekly or as required. If a child with a cold plays with a toy, that toy must be washed (sanitized) the same day.

For water play containers/sensory tables

The water play containers/sensory tables are filled with fresh water and sanitized at the end of each day the water table is in use.  Children with cuts, sores etc. on their hands should not be permitted to use a communal water play containers /sensory table.  Cups and other open vessels should not be used so that children are discouraged from drinking water in the water play container/sensory table.

Cots:

Cots and mats are disinfected bi-weekly or as required. Blankets are washed weekly or as they become soiled or as required by a staff member or a volunteers/students.

Linens:

Sheets on the children’s cots are washed- bi-weekly as required. Blankets are washed weekly or as they become soiled or as required when not in use (when no children use cot or spot vacancy). If a child is sent home ill, the sheets and blankets are washed immediately and the cot is disinfected by the staff immediately.

Garbage:

All garbage and kitchen food scraps are removed daily. Diapers are to be placed and kept in a closed container in the washroom and disposal at the end of the day, outside in the bins.

Infant/Toddler Toys:

Staff are to sanitize daily toys that the toddlers have placed in the mouths or if used by a child with a bad cold or running nose. (Rotate toys while others are being cleaned)

Toddler Diaper Procedure (pee, poop)

No more than five children (toddlers age group) sitting on carpet, on the floor, with book or small toy in the washroom/change area (during washroom routine).

Staff wash hands put on gloves.

Collect all items from child’s cubicle.

Placed child on change table on sanitized change pad surface.

Child must be put lying down immediately (child may have small toy while changing)

No standing on change table. (Follow diaper procedure)

Clean the child with wipes as instructed by the parent.

Dispose of each and every wipe into the diaper bin.

In the event that a child cannot be diapered accordingly (i.e. allergy), parents will asked to provide the necessary items (i.e. special wash cloths, paper towels, etc.) Such items should be put into plastic bag (Always Labeled), then given to the parent to take home. All children’s soiled clothing are put in plastic bag (Labeled) to be laundered at home by parents.

Do not turn away from the child; keep one hand on the child’s body at all times, wash child’s hands when change routine is complete.

Disinfect change pad as each child is done diapering, change gloves after each child’s turn.

Washroom diaper and toilet routine:

Children are to wash their hands with soap and water, after they have been to washroom routine. They dry their hands with a disposable paper towel. Dispose the used paper towel in the garbage provided in the washroom. Sanitize toilet seat after routine is complete. (Using disinfection solution)

Teachers/Staff/Home Providers:

In playroom teachers wash/sanitize hands after wiping children’s noses.

Lunch routine:

Home Providers, Home Visitors (if present and participate in lunch during visit), Persons Ordinarily on the Premises (if present and participate in lunch) and Children in care are to wash hands/mouth thoroughly before and after lunch.

Other Hygienic Practices:

Home Providers, Home Visitors and Persons Ordinarily on the Premises are to practice proper hand-washing procedures set out by the municipality public health, after completing washroom routine for themselves and or with the children; the same procedures are followed when agency children are on the child care site. Proper hand washing routines are followed before and after handling foods. When caring for a “sick child,” Providers should wash hands with soap and water prior to taking temperature and afterwards to prevent germs from spreading. Sanitize thermometer after each use.

Before and after administering first aid, staff must wash her/his hands thoroughly with soap and warm water thoroughly. Home Providers are required to wear disposable gloves and mask when treating a child who is bleeding, vomiting, diarrhea or requires any form of first aid.

Playground Area:

Home Providers are responsible for ensuring that the children tidy up the toys in the play area prior to coming inside. Paved areas should be swept daily and the yard should be litter free. Outdoor toys are washed as required. Ensure that a box of tissue paper and hand sanitizer are available when needed. Empty garbage bin when necessary.
