All Child Care Solutions Inc.

Disinfection Policy and Sanitary Procedures

To ensure the health and safety of all children and care givers the following disinfecting guidelines and procedures will be taken:

Hand Washing:

Please wash hands before and after preparation of food, touching a cut or open sore or touching eyes,

nose and or mouth. Wash hands after sneezing, coughing and blowing nose.

Wet hands with warm water and apply soap

Rub together vigorously for 15 seconds

Scrub entire hand, including back of hands between fingers and under nails.

Rinse and dry with paper towel

Use towel to turn off taps.

Diaper changing:

Wash hands

Place the individual child on the changing pad

Dispose of the soiled diaper or training pants in a diaper pail. Be sure that the pins are out of the child’s reach (if any)

Clean the child and apply a treatment ointment if provided and recommended by parents apply a fresh diaper or training pants.

Disinfect the child’s changing pad by spraying it with disinfecting solution (1 part household chlorine bleach to 100 parts water) and allowing 30-60 seconds contact time.

Dispose of the paper towels

Wash the child’s hands and take the child back to the playroom or seated near you.

Return the child’s clean changing pad into the child’s diaper compartment

Disinfect the change table using the disinfecting solution (follow steps in number 5.)

10. Wash hands thoroughly with soap and warm water.

Note: Diaper pails for disposable diapers should have a tight fitting lid and should be emptied and disinfected at least twice a day.

Disposable diapers should be double wrapped for disposal.

General disinfection:

Each day the following will be performed:

Toddler’s Area (mixed age group):

Disinfect countertops and changing table

Disinfect cabinet knobs

Disinfect door handle

Disinfect toilets

Carpet to be vacuumed at the end of the day

Floor to be washed and disinfected at the end of the day or when necessary during the day

Mouthed toys to be washed daily

Sensory water play table

Tables and chairs to be disinfected weekly

Preschool Area (mixed age group):

Disinfect countertops disinfect all door knobs

Disinfect hand rails if applicable

Carpet to be vacuumed at the end of the day

Floor to be washed and disinfected at the end of the day or when necessary during the day

Toys to be disinfected weekly

Tables and chairs to be disinfected after each use.

Washroom:

Disinfect door knobs

Disinfect sinks

Disinfect counter tops

Disinfect paper towel dispenser

Disinfect toilets

Wash floor and disinfect

Kitchen:

Disinfect counter tops

Disinfect appliances

Disinfect cabinet knobs

Disinfect door handle disinfect sinks

Wash floor and disinfect

All disinfection will be done with disposable paper towel using disinfecting solution available in the storage room/area away from children. Change the paper towel when finished with one area. This will optimize cleanliness and lessen the chances of sickness and spread of communicable diseases.

High touch surfaces must be disinfected after each used.

Caregivers will keep a General disinfection record upon completion.
