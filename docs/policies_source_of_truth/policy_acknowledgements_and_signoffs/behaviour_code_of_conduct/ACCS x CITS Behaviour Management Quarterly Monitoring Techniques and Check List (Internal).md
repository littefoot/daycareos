*Inappropriate behaviours are dealt
with using the following methods:
• Positive Intervention
• Firm/positive Communication
• Appropriate Body language
*Appropriate behaviours are recognized
with positive reinforcement
*Expectations/Guidelines are made
clear, limits set. Do not keep calling
child’s name as of he/she is a problem child 
*Children are encouraged to take
responsibility for their own actions and
problem solve when appropriate
*It is the behaviours, not the child,
which is identified as
inappropriate/appropriate
*Sit out is used as last chance to child
Staff must report to Supervisor when
sit out are used. Too many sit outs
are recorded in teacher’s file.
*Staff refrain from using the following
discipline techniques:
• Yelling
• Belittling
• Corporal Punishment
*Models attitudes and behaviour
patterns expected of children
*Exercises patience, remains calm in
directing behaviour or relaying
information repeatedly
