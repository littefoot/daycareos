All Child Care Solutions Inc.

Program Statement Implementation Policy

All Home Child Care Solutions Inc. is committed to ensuring that the practices set out in the program statement will be implemented by all staff, providers, volunteers and students.

PURPOSE

All Child Care Solutions Inc. Uses the Ministry of Education’s, How Does Learning Happen framework as a guide in developing our Program Statement. ACCS Program Statement will be reviewed annually by the Executive Director to ensure it aligns with the Ministry policy requirements.

PROGRAM STATEMENT REVIEW

All staff, providers, students and volunteers and any family members at the home that are working directly with children will review and sign this program statement policy prior to starting work or whenever the program statement is modified and annually thereafter.

SUPPORT - Staff, Providers, Volunteers and Students:

Staff will access support in the community to support them to implement the goals identified in the program statement. This may be accomplished through meetings, and professional training opportunities. Providers will be supported by ACCS staff through workshops to implement the goals identified in the program statement.

Prohibited Practices

ACCS wants to ensure each child has a safe and positive experience that promotes their healthy growth and development. Home Visitors will observe provider interactions with children ensuring that they align with our program statement and beliefs in adult-child interactions. The following practices are prohibited at all times in our provider’s homes:

A) Corporal punishment of the child;

B) Physical restraint of the child, such as confining the child to a high chair, car seat, stroller or other device for the purposes of discipline or in lieu of supervision, unless the physical restraint is for the purpose of preventing a child from hurting himself, herself or someone else, and is used only as a last resort and only until the risk of injury is no longer imminent;

C) Locking the exits of a home child care premises for the purpose of confining the child, or confining the child in an area or room without adult supervision, unless such confinement occurs during an emergency and is required as part of the licensee’s emergency management policies and procedures;

D) Use of harsh or degrading measures or threats or use of derogatory language directed at or used in the presence of a child that would humiliate, shame or frighten the child or undermine his or her self-respect, dignity or self-worth;

E) Depriving the child of basic needs including food, drink, shelter, sleep, toilet use, clothing or bedding; or

F) Inflicting any bodily harm on children including making children eat or drink against their will.

Monitoring and contravention

Failure to comply with the Program Statement will result in a staff/provider /volunteer/student meeting with the Executive Director to review the Program Statement and address any areas that are unclear or that the staff/provider /volunteer/student has trouble implementing.

The Executive Director and/or Home Visitor will offer materials, ideas and resources to help. Documentation will be made, indicating what was discussed and how the issue will be resolved. Both parties will sign and date a form which will remain in their file for 3 years.

Consultants will monitor and observe Providers for any prohibited practices, and make a comment in visits regarding adherence to the Program Statement and Prohibited Practices. If any contraventions are noted, they will put a summary in their visit notes, as well as discuss the situation with their Executive Director. Follow up will occur as outlined above.

If a staff/provider/volunteer/student continues to breech the Prohibited Practices, they may be terminated and home may will be closed.
