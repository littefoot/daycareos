All Child Care Solutions Inc.

Waiting List Procedure

Call, e-mail, fill in the online application of All Child Care Solutions Inc.

Each region has its own individual waiting list.

The day that you call, leave a message or send email we will place your child’s name on the waitlist according to seniority

Provide the required information for your file on the waiting list: Be mindful and strategic of your expected start month, you may also put the earliest start month you will consider for e.g. You are willing to start in march but you need a space for May.

If there are any changes to your contact information please contact the organization to update your file.

Spaces are created when a family or child leaves the organization/home care site. There is no specific time however the months of July, August and September have the most movement, Spaces can be available at any time of the year and at any point in the month.

We are usually able to contact families 4-6 weeks before any given space, as parents are only required to give us 1 months notice of their withdrawal.

Only once the withdrawal is confirmed, we can begin to find a family to fill the space according to seniority on the waiting list.

If a particular private home space is available or requested; the director will call families that are eligible or interested (based on child care site location) and follow order of seniority to offer the particular spot. All parents who are waiting for a particular month are contacted when spaces are available. Even if the month you specified has already passed.

Once a family is called from the waiting list they are given a specified time frame to return the call and express continued interest in the space available.

From the families that return our call within the specified time, the family with the highest seniority date will have first official approval of the available space.

As time moves along If a child is offered a space for a certain age group prior to enrollment we will inform you the likely hood of them transitioning to the next age grouping spot. For e.g. children less than 18 months but close to the 18-month mark; there may or may not be a space for them pending licensing regulations for in home care however; we will be able to give you a sense of if and when they will be able to start and the possibility. Since our enrollment can vary from month to month it is hopeful we will be able to find an alternative placing for a child in these instances.

For all children accepted into All Child Care Solutions Inc. there is a $50 registration fee. We do not charge to be put on the waitlist.

For all families that express continued interest in the space and are not successful; they will be contacted to let them know that another family has filled the space.

The waiting list will be made available in a manner that maintains the privacy and confidentiality of the children listed on it, this allows the position of a child on the list to be ascertained only by the affected persons or families. We do not release information about other families’ spots in the waiting list. All Records and documentation are kept in a secure location for a minimum of 3 years from the time they are made.
