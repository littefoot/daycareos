THE OZONE AND SUN CARE FOR YOUR CHILD  
The ozone layer is a laye r of atmospheric gases that surrounds the earth we live on.  This “blanket of gases” 
protects us
 from the sun’s powerful ultraviolet rays, which are extremely damaging to life on this planet including 
the lives
 of our children. Yet, even  now the ozone layer is slowly thinning due to certain chemicals present in out air. 
Industrial chemicals, such as CFCs (chlorofluorocarbons), have been eroding the earth’s ozone layer for the past 
decade.  These chemicals were recently present in many aerosol cans and Styrofoam products but as a result of increased 
awareness throughout our society, these chemicals have been reduced.  Also the exhaust fumes from all our cars and buses 
have contributed to the destruction of our ozone layer.  This is truly concerning as we s truggle with the idea of trying to 
locate environmentally friendly means of transportation.  
Many scientists are concerned that our world will be greatly affected should the ozone layer continue to 
deteriorate.  They are worried that the number of skin cancer incidents as well as severe sunburns, directly caused by the 
sun’s damaging ultraviolet rays, will continue to rise.  
We can watch for products that are “ozone friendly” as well as those products that aren’t.  If we work together to 
help our ozone layer, then our world will be a safer place for us and our children.  
Because of the thinning of our ozone layer, the sun’s harmful rays are penetrating the layer more easily.  This 
gives everyone cause for concern as incidents of skin cancer as well as sev ere sunburns are not fun!  These can be avoided 
by following a few simple steps in order to keep everyone healthy and happy.
