All Child Care Solutions Inc.

STAFF TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT POLICY

Purpose/Intent:

To provide guidelines in regards to All Child Care Solutions Inc. training and development.

Section 58 (1)

Policy:

At All Child Care Solutions Inc. we support our staff in relation to continuous professional learning, as we understand that staff enters employment with varying levels of knowledge, skills and experience. We believe that employees at All Child Care Solutions Inc. must have an understanding of what is required of their work. As such, in order to ensure that our staff is able to provide the children we serve with the best care possible, staff at All Child Care Solutions Inc. are given opportunities to acquire information to support the continuous upgrades and improvements.

Procedure:

The Following statements outline All Child Care Solutions Inc. policy in regards to staff training and development:

* At the commencement of their commitment to All Child Care Solutions Inc., all staff members and volunteers are required to participate in orientation. This program allows the supervisor/director to brief the staff member/volunteer about the operations of the agency. Following the orientation, staff/volunteers are required to review and sign off the policies and procedures manual.

* Staff members are provided with regular training workshops. These workshops include, but are not limited to, standard First Aid infant and child CPR and professional training through Resource Education.

* Qualification upgrades are encouraged to our teachers through E.C.E courses and specialized training in various child care issues regarding child development.

* Ongoing professional training is provided through regular staff meetings engaging staff to reflect about pedagogy for the early years and daily practices through discussion and collaborative inquiries among teams and other community members.

* All staff is encouraged to use city resources such as "Every Child Belongs" and enroll themselves in workshops of interest in order to enhance their professional development.

* All staff members are required to participate in two or more training workshops every year. The record of their attendance of such workshop is kept in their personnel file.

* All staff is encouraged to use the "Early Years Portal". This online resource is a valuable asset for all individuals as it provides overviews of licensing standards and allows staff to understand the requirement and expectations of licensed childcare programs.

* All staff is encouraged to use the "How Does Learning Happen" online resource to gain insight which will allow them grow both individually and as a team.

* R.E.C.E staff is encouraged to use the "Continuous Professional Learning" online resource to plan and document their professional learning in a meaningful way.
