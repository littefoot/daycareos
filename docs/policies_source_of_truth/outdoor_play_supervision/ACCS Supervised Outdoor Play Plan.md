All Child Care Solutions Inc.  
Supervised Outdoor Play Plan. 
   Providers do not take children swimming or near any standing  water 
Approved by provider, parent and home visitor. 
Our providers live in very nice neighbourhoods with lots of parks, water play areas, library and 
many other outdoor play areas. Yes lots of wonderful places to visit by walking. 
The Agency and providers feel that frequent walks with the children and mini outings are a very 
important part of the provider’s daily program and schedule. These outings are a lot of fun time 
for children and providers. Social outings form great experience within their learning 
environment and language development. (see the butterflies, hear the birds, look at squirrels etc.). 
It is therefore important that the provider along with the parents and home visitor create an 
outdoor play plan where there is open space within a safe play area. Everyone can have peace of 
mind and know that the children play safe and are very well supervised. 
During walks or outdoor play it is important that the children experience a range of gross motor 
activities, enabling child(ren) to develop their gross motor skills such is climbing, jumping, 
crawling, skipping, exercising and more. (not always available indoors) 
Outdoor play and walks are heart healthy for kids and for their physical growth. 
It is also important that parents are aware of what developmental activities their children are 
taking part in. 
We therefore; are asking parents to help in planning these activities along with the provider 
and home visitors. 
Since the providers visit different places like backyards, parks, water play facilities and 
libraries, etc; Providers should have an outdoor plan and schedule of where they are going and 
what they are doing with the children on a daily basis. i.e. If the provider is going to stay in the 
backyard, what activities for outdoor could be planned suitable for the back yard. 
Outdoor play plan can be planned as followed. 
1. Program plan/statement related. 6. Games related.
2. Season related. 7. Transportation related.
3. Holidays related. 8. Movement (music) related.
4. Environment related. 9. Dramatic related.
5. Special occasion days related. 10. The world around us related.
Interest related: eg - Animals.
a. Animals at home (pets)
b. Animals at the farm.
c. Animals in the forest
IMPORTANT. 
Provider and children are not allowed near or 
by standing bodies of water, i.e. beach, lake, 
pool, pond, jacuzzi, wading pool etc. 
d. Animals in the water.
We can pretend to be animals. 
Outdoor play plan can be created with or without equipment. 
Available equipment for the backyard: sandbox, bikes, hoola hoops, balls, chalkboard, 
crawl tunnel, backyard toys, equipment must be washed regularly. 
Available equipment in the park: swing, climber, water play, monkey bar, slide. (must be age appropriate)
Make sure they are safe before using them with the children. Providers must stand close to the large 
play ground equipment for child’s safety and well being.
