# All Child Care Solutions (ACCS) — Expiry Documents, Monitoring & Notification System

**Document Version**: 1.0.0  
**Effective Date**: August 2026 / CCEYA Standard  
**Governing Authority**: Ontario Ministry of Education, Child Care and Early Years Act (CCEYA, 2014), Public Health Ontario, and Regional Municipalities (Durham, York, Toronto).

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
This document establishes the official regulatory compliance matrix, automated expiration tracking timelines, notification escalation protocols, and ratio calculation engines for **All Child Care Solutions (ACCS)**.

The system deterministically monitors:
1. **Licensed Home Child Care Providers**: Police checks, medical clearances, insurance, contracts, household adult member clearances, and tax deadlines.
2. **Home Child Care Visitors (RECE)**: Professional licensing with the College of ECE, field safety credentials, and training certifications.
3. **Enrolled Children**: Sabrina's Law (anaphylaxis/Epi-Pens), Ryan's Law (asthma inhalers), prescription medications, immunization yellow cards, legal custody/court orders, special permissions, and daily morning attendance check-ins.
4. **Parents & Financial Timelines**: CWELCC/Private fee payment cycles, gentle reminders, strict 3-day post-due termination escalation warnings, and municipal subsidy renewal deadlines.
5. **CCEYA Mixed-Age Ratios**: Enforcing statutory maximums (6 children total, 3 under 3 years old, 3 under 2 years old), deduction of provider's own children under 4 years old, and school-age (6–12 years) enrollment rules.

## 2. Licensed Provider Compliance Matrix

All licensed home child care providers operate as independent contractors under an agency agreement. The following documents must be tracked with automated alert intervals:

| Compliance Document | Statutory Lifespan / Expiry | Warning Alert Window | Hard Escalation Window | Action Required When Expired |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vulnerable Sector Check (VSC)** | **5 Years** | 60 days & 30 days before expiry | 14 days before expiry | Care suspension notice; provider cannot operate without valid police check. |
| **Annual Offence Declaration** | **1 Year** (Annual) | 30 days before 12-month mark | 7 days before | Mandatory digital signature under CCEYA; suspension if overdue. |
| **Standard First Aid & CPR Level C** | **3 Years** | 60 days & 30 days before expiry | 14 days before | Recertification with St. John Ambulance / Red Cross / Lifesaving Society. |
| **Food Handler Certification** | Optional / Best Practice | 30 days before expiry | At expiration | Local Public Health department certification renewal. |
| **Tetanus Immunization (Td / Tdap)** | **10 Years** | 90 days & 30 days before expiry | At expiration | Medical booster record from family physician. |
| **TB Clearance / Chest X-Ray** | **2 Years** (or baseline negative) | 60 days & 30 days before expiry | At expiration | Updated 1-step/2-step TB test or chest X-ray physician sign-off. |
| **MMR Immunization** | Variable / Verified on file | N/A (One-time or titer) | Missing on file | Proof of 2 doses or laboratory evidence of immunity. |
| **Independent Contractor Agreement** | **1 Year** (Annual Renewal) | 30 days before annual date | 7 days before | Contract review, fee rate schedule verification, mutual re-execution. |
| **Home Liability Insurance Rider** | **1 Year** (Annual Renewal) | 45 days & 15 days before expiry | 3 days before | $2,000,000 to $5,000,000 commercial daycare liability certificate naming ACCS. |
| **T4A Annual Tax Information Slip** | **Annual (Last working day of Feb)** | Alert on Feb 1 (28 days prior) | Alert on Feb 20 (7 days prior) | Agency must generate and deliver T4A tax slip to provider before month end. |
| **Google Drive Video Compliance Training** | **Annual Policy Sign-off** | 30 days before annual renewal | At annual renewal | Provider must watch short-form compliance videos and e-sign annual policy. |

### Provider Household Adult Members (Family Members 18+)
Under Ontario CCEYA Section 54, every individual aged 18 or older who resides in the home must have full compliance documentation on file:
1. **Vulnerable Sector Check (Police Background Check)**: Expiry every **5 years**.
2. **Annual Offence Declaration**: Expiry every **1 year**.
3. **Behaviour Management Policy Sign-Off**: Mandatory annual signed acknowledgement that no corporal punishment or prohibited practices occur in the home.

## 5. Parent Financial, Fee & Subsidy Escalation Protocols

### Monthly CWELCC & Private Parent Fee Reminders
- **Monthly Due Date**: On or before the **1st calendar day of each month**.
- **T-Minus 3 Days (28th/29th of Month)**: **Gentle Reminder** dispatched via notification/email:
  > *"Gentle reminder: Your monthly child care fee of [Amount] for [Child Name] is due on or before the 1st of next month. Thank you for choosing All Child Care Solutions!"*
- **1st Day of Month**: **Due Date Notice** (if unpaid):
  > *"Reminder: Monthly fee payment of [Amount] is due today. Please submit via Interac e-transfer to allchildcaresolutions@gmail.com."*
- **T-Plus 3 Days (4th Day of Month)**: **Strict Overdue Escalation & Termination Warning**:
  > *"URGENT: Monthly fee payment of [Amount] for [Child Name] is now 3 days overdue. In accordance with ACCS Parent Fee Policy, if payment is not received within 3 days after the 1st of the month, child care placement may be terminated immediately. Please contact the office immediately."*

### Municipal Child Care Subsidy Monitoring (Durham, York, Toronto)
- **60 Days Before Expiry**: Notify parent to submit income verification to municipal case worker.
- **30 Days Before Expiry**: Request confirmation of renewed subsidy authorization letter.
- **14 Days Before Expiry**: Critical warning to prevent lapse to full-fee private billing ($35.00/day).

## 6. CCEYA Mixed-Age Capacity & Ratio Engine Rules

Under the Ontario Child Care and Early Years Act (CCEYA, 2014) for Licensed Home Child Care:
1. **Total Children Maximum**: **No more than 6 children** total may be present in the home at any one time.
2. **Infants Under 2 Years**: **No more than 3 children under 2 years of age** are permitted.
3. **Toddlers Under 3 Years**: Standard safe grouping ensures high supervision for young learners.
4. **Provider's Own Children Under 4 Years Old**:
   - Any child of the provider under 4 years of age **must be counted** towards the total capacity of 6 and reduces the number of placement vacancies available to agency clients.
5. **School-Age Children (6 to 12 Years Old)**:
   - The provider can configure whether their home accepts school-age children (before/after school programs, PA days, and summer care).
   - School-age children count toward the total 6-child cap during hours of attendance.

## 7. Google Drive Compliance Training Architecture
To streamline annual policy sign-offs and regulatory orientations for Independent Providers and Home Visitors:
- A centralized Google Drive video repository link (`complianceVideoUrl`) is stored and configurable for each provider and visitor profile.
- Direct UI action: `[ 📹 Watch Compliance Videos (Google Drive) ]` opens the video repository in a secure new tab.
- Integrated Policy Sign-Off toggle records the completion date and resets the 1-year compliance countdown timer.
