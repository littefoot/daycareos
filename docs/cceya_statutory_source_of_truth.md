# Ontario Child Care & Early Years Act (CCEYA) — Canonical Source of Truth

> **Authoritative Provincial Sources**:
> 1. **Statute**: [Child Care and Early Years Act, 2014, S.O. 2014, c. 11](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/14c11)
> 2. **Licensing Regulation**: [Ontario Regulation 137/15: General](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/13715)
> 3. **Operational Manual**: [Ministry of Education Home Child Care Licensing Manual](https://www.ontario.ca/page/home-child-care-licensing-manual)

---

## 1. Statutory Ratio & Capacity Standards (O. Reg. 137/15 s. 8)

### Master Headcount Limits:
1. **Total Premises Limit (s. 8(1))**:
   - A home child care provider may provide home child care for no more than **6 children** at any one time.
   - **Provider's Own Children**: Any child of the provider younger than 4 years of age (or younger than 6 years of age under agency contractual policy) who is present on the premises MUST be counted directly toward the maximum capacity of 6.
2. **Age Group Stratification (s. 8(2))**:
   - **Under 3 Years Old (< 36 months)**: No more than **3 children** younger than three years of age.
   - **Under 2 Years Old (< 24 months)**: No more than **3 children** younger than two years of age.
   *(Note: Under O. Reg. 137/15 s. 8(2), a provider is legally permitted to care for up to 3 infants under 2 years of age, provided the total under-3 limit of 3 is not exceeded).*

## 2. Home Child Care Visitor Statutory Mandate (CCEYA s. 26 & O. Reg. 137/15 s. 52)

1. **Qualifications**:
   - Every home child care visitor employed by a licensed agency must be a member in good standing of the **College of Early Childhood Educators (RECE)**.
2. **Mandatory Unannounced Quarterly Inspections**:
   - Unannounced quarterly inspections must occur at every affiliated home child care premises at least **once every 90 days**.
3. **Audit Protocol**:
   - **Physical Headcount Reconciliation**: In-person headcount of all children present, categorized by age (<2, <3, 3–6, 6+), compared directly against the provider's active digital daily attendance record.
   - **Environmental Safety**: First aid kits, poisonous substances locked, smoke and carbon monoxide detectors tested, heating/cooling compliance.
   - **Medical & Anaphylaxis Files**: Active Epi-Pens within manufacturer expiry dates, posted anaphylaxis alert sheets with child photos.
   - **Sleep Supervision Records**: Physical sign-off confirming direct visual sleep checks conducted and logged.
   - **Tamper-Evident Certification**: Completed inspection reports must be signed by both the RECE Home Visitor and the Provider, synchronized with the agency licensee records.

## 3. Serious Occurrence Reporting (O. Reg. 137/15 s. 38)

- **Mandatory Reporting Window**: Licensee must submit a Serious Occurrence report to the Ministry of Education Child Care Licensing System (CCLS) within **24 hours** of becoming aware of the incident.
- **Reportable Categories**:
  1. Death of a child.
  2. Unplanned hospital admission or serious injury requiring medical attention.
  3. Allegation of abuse or neglect.
  4. Missing or unsupervised child.
  5. Disaster, fire, or evacuation on premises.

---

## 4. Medication & Medical Administration (O. Reg. 137/15 s. 40)

- **Parental Consent**: Written authorization from parent required before administering any drug or medication (prescription or non-prescription).
- **Storage**: Medications must be locked in a container inaccessible to children, except emergency anaphylaxis epinephrine injectors which must be easily accessible to adults but out of children's reach.
- **Administration Log**: Every dose administered must be immediately recorded with date, time, dosage, child name, and provider signature.
