7.  Monthly Child Care Fee Payment 
Parents/guardians are required to pay all applicable child care fees in full on the first (1st) day of each 
month, in accordance with the Agency’s established Fee Schedule. If full payment has not been received 
by the Agency by the third (3rd) day of the month, child care services will be suspended and the child’s 
space may be considered forfeited. The Agency reserves the right to offer the space to another family 
requiring care. Parents/guardians are responsible for ensuring that payments are submitted on time to avoid 
any interruption or loss of child care services. Please refer to the Fee Schedule for applicable monthly 
child care charges, payment methods, and payment instructions.

8. Private Child Care Arrangements and Non-Circumvention 
Parents/guardians and child care providers introduced, matched, placed, or otherwise connected through the 
Agency are strictly prohibited from entering into any private child care arrangement with one another 
outside of the Agency while either party is receiving or providing services through the Agency. This restriction 
will continue for a period of three (3) years following the last date on which the parent/guardian received 
child care services or the provider provided child care services through the Agency, whichever is later. All 
child care arrangements between a parent/guardian and an Agency-affiliated provider must be arranged, 
authorized, and administered through the Agency. A parent/guardian and provider may not privately continue, 
transfer, negotiate, or establish child care services for the purpose of bypassing the Agency or its contractual 
arrangements. A breach of this provision may result in the immediate termination of Agency services. In 
addition, each party participating in an unauthorized private child care arrangement may be liable to the 
Agency for contractual damages in an amount of not less than $3,000.00 and not limited to $3,000.00, and 
the Agency may seek additional amounts, subject to applicable law, together with any other remedies 
available to the Agency. Where necessary, the Agency reserves the right to pursue appropriate legal remedies 
through the courts for a breach of this provision.
