Policy, Practices, Rules and Regulations for Day Care Providers 
According to the C.C.E.Y.A. and Municipal By-Laws 
Anyone may apply to become a home-based caregiver regardless of their status; however the Agency must 
follow through their approval procedures many laws. (Providers are not employees) 
(Providers are independent contractors) 
      The Agency operates 7 days per week and provides 24 hours care to children 
1. Providers may be of any nationality, but must hold a work permit and Social Insurance Number.
2. If you are on Employment Insurance (E.I.) your income must be reported.
3. You may be of any kind of religion but you may not discuss or influence your religion upon parents, other 
providers or have discussions about this while children are being cared for in your home.
4. To be a caregiver for children you must be an adult with your OWN place (house or apartment) apartment 
building height restrictions apply so there is no interference and/or disruptions in the quality care you 
provide. (One can not be living with other people: i.e. Shared accommodations or in a rented basement. 
Monthly meetings are open to all registered providers who are members of the Agency.
Provider’s family members/relatives who are not a member of the agency but have interest in Early 
Childhood Education and are willing to learn about children (pending space availability) may request a visit 
to our meetings. Parents who are registered with the Agency or future parents who wish to join the agency 
may also request a visit. (Space/Time restrictions apply)
A charge to attend a meeting, workshop (fees do include materials), information session or learning session 
applies. Meetings are held usually on a Saturday morning. (See meeting schedule).
Hiring Policy of the Agency: (Provider or in home family members cannot violate ANY Laws).
A provider must attend the monthly meetings in order to take children in their home.
A person over 21 years of age (preferably); a parent who is experienced with their own children and living 
in a typical family setting. We are looking for family oriented childcare.
We will give considerations to others please note, that parents are looking for ample space for their children 
to play and thrive. A limited amount of people should be involved in the child’s (active day). Parents prefer 
consistency, same caregiver, the same home and a strong rapport with the same person in the home. Usually 
the caregiver (not with their family members)
Only one person is in charge of the children. The provider only.
5. Every adult living in the home must complete a medical form. Adult forms are completed by the doctor 
with 
Doctor’s stamp. (Doctor completes Agency form).
Provider’s children’s medicals are completed by the caregiver (up to date vaccinations are required).
A provider cannot start care until all medicals of the whole family are received.
6. Everyone over the age of 18 years must have a criminal reference check and or vulnerable sector check 
completed by municipal police.
The police will charge a fee for reference check and fingerprinting. Each municipality is different.
A provider cannot provide care for children until the returned police checks are received.
Agency signature is required on Toronto Police Papers. (Toronto Police clearance will be rejected without 
Agency signature).
Durham Region and York Region; Ask Agency first about police clearance. We only accept police 
clearance from the Police services where you live.

7. Adults over 18 years of age and persons ordinarily resident on the premises must sign a Behavior
Management form; stating that they are not to engage in ANY Prohibited Practices. Agency forms are
completed and signed annually.
8. The caregiver’s home must be approved by the Agency first. (Providers must comply with all Agency
requirements).
a. You must have a clean and safe home complete with:
b. Smoke detectors on each floor (if electric, then you will need one battery operated detector as back
up)
c. Carbon Monoxide detectors on each floor. These detectors must be tested on a monthly basis. A good
time to do this is when you do your monthly fire drill with the children. Record this information in
your daily logbook provided by the agency.
d. Fire extinguisher (5 lbs.)
e. Providers must have updated first aid training and a first aid kit with manual. (Epi-pen training)
Agency holds first aid trainings as required.
f. If any one has a license for a firearm in the home; then you must give a copy of the license to
the Agency for your file and whenever there are changes regarding your fire arm registration.
g. All chemicals and medication, dangerous objects, adult-materials, books, videos, etc. etc. Must
be put away out of children’s reach. We are looking for high quality childcare homes, suitable
for young children. We and YOU (the provider) want our parents to be happy.
9. The homes are open (24/7) to the Department of Social Services, Children’s Aid Society, The Ministry
of Education, The Fire Department and Public Health any time when care is provided, extended hours
7 days a week, evening care till 9.30 pm, sleepover nights and weekend care. Wherever care is provided.
When a child is in your home, the Agency staff and any of these Government Agencies can enter your home
without notice at any time. This is to ensure a provider’s home is always in compliance with
the Law and Agency Rules and Regulations.
10.Providers must sign a contract with the Agency on a yearly basis so that you know the Agency will pay you
for your services (see payment schedule). You must
 also keep a record of attendance daily
(Supplied to you by the Agency). The Agency will help you step by step after your 6 weeks of training are
completed; and your medical and police checks are received. You will be ready to take care of
children in your home (post agency recommendations). 1 – 6 children maximum if approved including
your own children under 6 years of age; Municipality requirements are influenced and may differ depending
on your district.
11.Welcome to All Child Care Solutions INC.
(We do not guarantee children) You cannot work with two Agencies at the same time. (unless authorized)
Providers who wish to care for private children and also looking to join an agency are called referrals and
are not members of the agency (Contact us for further information).
Priority of placements for children is: for members of the agency only.
12.Membership. The Agency provides children to providers who have become a member of the Agency.
Day Care children are not guaranteed. (Parents choose providers by themselves) not the Agency.
The membership fee is onetime $400.00 - $500.00, which included 6 weeks training;
Several visits to providers home, setup help; telephone calls to the agency are recorded, tallied and
is part of the charges. Agency calling providers for phone training or Online training are also recorded,
tallied and is part of the charges. The Agency fee is $85.00 (subject to change pending conditions) per home
visit, to open a provider’s home. All charges are included when opening a home within the $400.00 -
$500.00 pricing structure (pending conditions of the home). Please make cheques payable to All Child Care
Solutions.
Sincerely, All Child Care Solutions Director.
