Regulatory Requirements: Ontario Regulation 137/15 
Child illness and accident 
36(4) Every licensee shall ensure that when a child receiving child care at a child care centre it operates 
or at a premises where it oversees the provision of home child care is injured, 
(a)a n accident report is made describing the circumstances of the injury and any first aid administered;
and
(b)a  copy of the report is provided to a parent of the child.
