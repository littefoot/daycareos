4-Week Home Child Care Program Plan
Based on How Does Learning Happen? – Ontario’s Pedagogy for the Early Years 
This simple program plan is designed for home child care providers working with mixed-age 
children. Activities are easy, low-prep, and use items you already have at home. 
Inspired by guidance from the Ontario Ministry of Education’s How Does Learning Happen? 
framework, focusing on: 
• Belonging – Feeling safe and included
• Well-Being – Healthy bodies and emotions
• Engagement – Curiosity and play
• Expression – Communication and creativity
WEEK 1 – Getting to Know Each Other 
(Belonging) 
Interest: Friends & Family 
Day Easy Activity What Children Learn (HDLH 
Connection) 
Monday Show-and-Tell: children bring or talk about 
favourite toy Belonging – children feel valued 
Tuesday Draw family with crayons Expression – sharing their world 
Wednesday Group snack prep (wash fruit together) Well-Being – hygiene + teamwork 
Thursday Name song (sing each child’s name) Belonging + Language 
Friday Free play with dolls/figures Engagement – pretend play 
Special Event Idea: Family photo wall 
Provider Tip: Ask open questions like "Who is in your home?" or "What makes you happy?"

WEEK 2 – Exploring Our Senses (Engagement) Interest: Touch, Taste, Smell, Sound, Sight Day Easy Activity What Children Learn Monday Sensory bin with rice or pasta Engagement – exploring textures Tuesday Taste test: sweet vs salty snack Language – describing words Wednesday Nature walk sounds Well-Being – outdoor calm Thursday Smell jars (vanilla, orange peel) Curiosity + memory Friday Colour hunt around house Cognitive – sorting & noticing Special Event Idea: Bubble day outside Provider Tip: Let children lead play – follow their interests.  WEEK 3 – Moving Our Bodies (Well-Being) Interest: Healthy Bodies Day Easy Activity What Children Learn Monday Obstacle course with pillows Gross motor skills Tuesday Dance party with scarves Expression + joy Wednesday Yoga stretch time Self-regulation Thursday Ball rolling & catching Coordination Friday Wash dolls & toys Caring habits Special Event Idea: Picnic lunch outside Provider Tip: Praise effort, not perfection.

WEEK 4 – Creativity & Imagination (Expression) Interest: Art Everywhere Day Easy Activity What Children Learn Monday Paint with sponges Creativity Tuesday Build with boxes Problem solving Wednesday Story time + act it out Language + confidence Thursday Sticker collage Fine motor Friday Music parade with homemade instruments Expression + teamwork Special Event Idea: Mini art show for parents Provider Tip: Ask children to explain their artwork.   Daily Routine Reminder (Simple Structure) Morning: Free play + circle Snack Activity Outdoor play Lunch Nap/Quiet time Afternoon play + story Pick-up
