INSTRUCTIONS 
Ontario Regulation 137/15 made under the Child Care and Early Years Act, 2014 (
CCEYA) requires that: 
26. (1) Every licensee of a home child care agency shall ensure that before a premises is used as a premises where the licensee
is to oversee the provision of home child care, the premises, including the outdoor play space, is inspected by a home child care 
visitor employed by the licensee to ensure compliance with the Act and this Regulation and, where the premises is so used, that 
further inspections are carried out without prior notice to the home child care provider, at least once in every quarter of each 
calendar year, and at such other times as the director may require. 
(2) The home child care visitor shall use any checklist provided by the director in performing an inspection of a home child care 
premises. 
The following checklist must be completed in its entirety for a
ll home child care premises inspections conducted by home visitors of 
a licensed home child care agency, before a premises is used to provide home child care or in-home services and, at a minimum, 
on a quarterly basis thereafter. The checklist must be fully completed by the end of each quarter. Home child care visitors may wish 
to conduct multiple inspections to a premises during a quarter and complete sections of the checklist at that time. 
The checklist questions that appear in the standard home visitor checklist reflect requirements under the CCEYA and O. Reg. 137/15 
only. Each checklist question has a corresponding risk level. Licensing requirements have four risk levels: 
• Critical: Non-compliance poses a direct threat to a child which could result in / has resulted in death
• High: Non-compliance poses a direct threat to a child which could result in /
 has res
ulted in serious harm to their health,
safety and well-being
• Moderate: Non-compliance poses an indirect threat to a child which could result in / has resulted in harm to the health, safety
and well-being of a child
• Low: Non-compliance is not as likely to pose a threat to the health, safety and well-being of children, but the possibility exists.

Completing the standard home visitor checklist is the minimum requirement under the CCEYA. The home child care agency licensee 
may supplement this checklist with additional checklists or items that reflect the requirements of local bylaws and the agency’s specific 
policies and procedures. 
The Ministry’s expectation is that the home child care visitor will inspect all the rooms used for the care of children, at a minimum. The 
licensee should determine whether additional rooms are to be inspected based on their use, accessibility to children and other safety 
concerns. Where the agency’s policies prohibit any provisions in the standard home visitor checklist, these items may be marked “N/A” 
as they will not apply. Where items cannot be observed at the time of the inspection, providers may be asked to verbally confirm their 
practices. 
For more information on requirements under the CCEYA and O. Reg. 137/15, please refer to the Home Child Care Licensing Manual 
and/or contact your Ministry program advisor. 
This Standard Home Visitor Checklist is current as of November 2023.
