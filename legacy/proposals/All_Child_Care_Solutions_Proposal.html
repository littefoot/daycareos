4. Core Platform Capabilities ($1,500 Scope)

📊 Centralized Staff CRM Dashboard

  • Family & Child Database: Single dashboard managing parents, children, medical info, and emergency contacts via Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Health & Allergy Tracking: Structured view of allergies, dietary needs, routines, and support requirements.
  • Document Management Center: Store, review, approve, or request replacements for birth certificates, health cards, and immunization records in Firebase Storage.
  • Interactive Email Dispatcher: Send interactive registration form links directly via email (Resend API) or direct URL.

🎮 Gamified Parent Registration Portal

  • Gamified Progress Map: Visual step-by-step progress tracking for parents from start to completion.
  • Save & Resume: Parents can complete forms in one sitting or save progress and return anytime.
  • Mobile-Responsive Uploads: Easy smartphone document capture for birth certificates and health cards.
  • 1-Click PDF Summary: Instant printable application summary page for family and agency records.
