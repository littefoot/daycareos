# Ontario Licensed Home Child Care Regulatory & Agency Policy Manual
**All Child Care Solutions Inc. (ACCS) & Circles in the Sun (CITS)**  
*Governed by the Child Care and Early Years Act, 2014 (CCEYA) & Ontario Regulation 137/15*

---

## 1. Statutory Authority & System Overview
All Child Care Solutions Inc. is an Ontario licensed Home Child Care Agency operating under the **Ministry of Education** and the **Child Care and Early Years Act, 2014 (CCEYA)** across **Durham Region** (Oshawa, Whitby, Ajax, Pickering, Clarington), **York Region** (Markham, Richmond Hill, Vaughan), and **Toronto**.

The agency contracts independent home child care providers who provide care in their private residences. The agency oversees regulatory compliance, safety, curriculum, admissions, fee subsidies, and provider compensation.

## 2. Home Child Care Visitor Mandate (CCEYA O. Reg. 137/15, s. 26)

### 2.1 Statutory Inspection Frequency
Under **O. Reg. 137/15, Section 26(1)**:
1. **Pre-Licensing / Initial Premises Approval**: Before any child care premises is used, the entire premises (including the outdoor play space and all rooms accessible to children) **must be inspected** by an agency Home Child Care Visitor to ensure full compliance with the Act and Regulation.
2. **Quarterly Unannounced Inspections**: Once a home is active, the Home Visitor **must conduct unannounced inspections at least once in every quarter of each calendar year** (Q1: Jan–Mar, Q2: Apr–Jun, Q3: Jul–Sep, Q4: Oct–Dec), and at such other times as the agency Director may require.
3. **Use of Standard Ministry Checklist**: Under Section 26(2), the visitor must use the official Ministry of Education *Standard Home Visitor Checklist* or the agency Director's approved equivalent.

### 2.2 Home Visitor Qualifications & Ratios
- **Professional Standard**: Home Child Care Visitors must be members in good standing with the **College of Early Childhood Educators (RECE)** or hold equivalent approved qualifications.
- **Agency Caseload Standard**: A full-time Home Visitor typically manages an active caseload of **15 to 25 licensed provider homes**, ensuring adequate supervision, coaching, and compliance monitoring.

### 2.3 Required In-Home Inspection Areas (Quarterly Audit)
Every quarterly visit must assess and document the following:
1. **Capacity & Headcount Verification**:
   - Physical headcount must be counted immediately upon entry and reconciled with the provider’s daily written attendance log.
2. **Health, Sanitary & Environmental Safety**:
   - Tested smoke detectors on each level and carbon monoxide alarms outside sleeping areas.
   - Fire extinguisher (2A-10BC minimum) fully charged, inspected, and easily accessible.
   - First aid kit fully stocked (adhesive bandages, sterile gauze, tape, tweezers, scissors, cold pack, CPR mask).
   - Hot water temperature capped at a maximum of **49°C (120°F)** to prevent scalding.
   - Electrical outlets capped with safety covers.
   - Hazardous chemicals, cleaning supplies, and toxic plants locked or completely inaccessible.
   - All medications (prescription and over-the-counter) secured in a locked lockbox or locked cupboard.
3. **Safe Sleep Standards (CCEYA s. 35)**:
   - Children under 18 months must be provided with an individual crib or cradle meeting Health Canada *Cribs, Cradles and Bassinets Regulations*.
   - No soft bedding, bumper pads, heavy blankets, or toys in cribs.
   - Direct visual sleep checks must be conducted and logged periodically.
4. **Nutrition & Food Handling (CCEYA s. 40)**:
   - Weekly menu posted (minimum 2 weeks in advance) meeting Canada’s Food Guide.
   - Drinking water available and accessible to children at all times.
   - Children's allergy list clearly posted in the food preparation area.
5. **Outdoor Play Space & Playground Safety (CCEYA s. 28)**:
   - Fenced outdoor play yard (minimum 1.2 m / 4 ft fence with self-closing and self-latching gate).
   - Free of hazards, debris, toxic weeds, animal feces, and standing bodies of water.
6. **Sabrina’s Law & Anaphylaxis Plans (CCEYA s. 36)**:
   - Individualized anaphylaxis plan with emergency protocol posted on the refrigerator for every child with an identified life-threatening allergy.
   - Unexpired Epi-Pen / auto-injector verified on site, kept in an easily accessible but child-safe location.
7. **Behaviour Guidance & Prohibited Practices (CCEYA s. 48)**:
   - Strict zero-tolerance for prohibited practices:
     - No corporal punishment of a child.
     - No physical restraint of a child, unless for preventing immediate harm.
     - No harsh, degrading, or humiliating measures (or derogatory language).
     - No confining or locking a child in any room or enclosure.
     - No deprivation of basic needs (food, water, bedding, clothing, toilet use).

## 3. CCEYA Group Composition & Ratio Limits (s. 8 & O. Reg. 137/15 s. 8(2))

### 3.1 Statutory Source of Truth
- **CCEYA Statute (2014, c. 11)**: [https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/14c11](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/14c11)
- **Home Child Care Licensing Manual (O. Reg. 137/15)**: [https://www.ontario.ca/page/home-child-care-licensing-manual](https://www.ontario.ca/page/home-child-care-licensing-manual)

### 3.2 Maximum Permissible Capacity
Under the CCEYA, no home child care provider may care for more than **6 children** in total at any given time, including the provider's **own children who are under four/six years of age**.

### 3.3 Strict Age Breakdown Sub-Limits
Within the maximum 6-child limit, the following statutory restrictions apply:
- **Maximum Under Age 2**: No more than **3 children** may be under 2 years of age (24 months).
- **Maximum Under Age 3**: No more than **3 children** may be under 3 years of age (36 months).
- **Provider's Own Children**: Every child of the provider who resides in the home and is young counts directly against the total 6-child capacity and against the under-2 or under-3 limits if applicable.

| Metric | Statutory Maximum | Rule Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Total Children (including provider's own young children) | **6 children** | CCEYA s. 8(1) |
| Children under 3 years old (<36 months) | **Max 3 children** | O. Reg. 137/15 s. 8(2)(b) |
| Children under 2 years old (<24 months) | **Max 3 children** | O. Reg. 137/15 s. 8(2)(a) |
| Provider's own children residing in home | Counted 1:1 against ratio | CCEYA s. 8(3) |

## 4. Canada-Wide Early Learning and Child Care (CWELCC) & Fee Policies

### 4.1 Parent Fee Structure
Ontario participates in CWELCC to reduce average parent fees for children under age 6:
- **Current CWELCC Phase**: **$22.00 / day** (average $110.00/week | **$478.50 / month** for full-time 5 days/week).
- **Upcoming Target Phase**: **$10.00 / day** (average $50.00/week | **$217.50 / month** for full-time 5 days/week).
- **Monthly Fee Standard Calculation**: `(Daily Rate × Days Per Week) × 4.35 Average Weeks per Month` (52 weeks ÷ 12 months = 4.333, standard rounded to 4.35).
- **Municipal Fee Subsidy (Durham, York, Toronto)**: Covers 100% of the parent portion for eligible families (**$0.00 / day** parent co-pay).
- **Unsubsidized Private Care Rate**: **$35.00 / day** ($761.25 / month).

### 4.2 Provider Compensation Guarantee
Regardless of whether a child is funded through private pay, municipal subsidy, or CWELCC:
- **Universal Statutory Payout**: **$35.00 / day per child** guaranteed to the licensed provider.
  - *CWELCC ($22 parent)*: Parent pays $22.00 + Provincial CWELCC grant pays $13.00 = **$35.00/day**.
  - *CWELCC ($10 target)*: Parent pays $10.00 + Provincial CWELCC grant pays $25.00 = **$35.00/day**.
  - *Municipal Subsidy*: Municipality pays full **$35.00/day** directly to agency.
  - *Private Care*: Parent pays full **$35.00/day**.

## 5. Attendance & Timesheet Reconciliation Standards

### 5.1 Daily Written Attendance Log (CCEYA s. 37)
- Providers must maintain a contemporaneous, written record of every child’s exact arrival time and departure time daily.
- Arrival and departure entries must be signed/initialed by parents daily or confirmed via the provider portal.

### 5.2 Varied Enrolment Timesheet Coding Standards
For varied or part-time schedules, ACCS enforces standard Ministry audit attendance codes:
- **SE (Sick on Scheduled Day)**: Insufficient notice given; payment is expected.
- **AE (Absent on Scheduled Day)**: Child did not attend; insufficient notice given; payment is expected.
- **CE (Time Changed / Schedule Variance)**: Time changed with insufficient notice; payment expected for scheduled hours.
- **NP (No Payment Expected)**: Scheduled closure, approved vacation notice, or holiday where payment is waived.
- **H (Statutory Holiday)**: Ontario statutory holiday recognized under provincial funding.

## 6. Standing Bodies of Water Policy (Agency Rule)
Under ACCS and CITS policy:
- No in-ground or above-ground swimming pools on premises may be accessed by daycare children during operating hours.
- Decorative ponds, wading pools, or standing water containers must be securely fenced with locked gates or emptied immediately after supervised sensory play.
- No children may swim in private residential pools during home child care hours.

## 7. Medical Needs & Sabrina’s Law (Sabrina's Law, 2005)
1. **Medical Form Requirement**: Every child with an allergy, asthma, seizure disorder, or medical diagnosis must have an *Individualized Medical Support Plan* completed prior to their first day of care.
2. **Staff & Provider Training**: Home Visitors must ensure the provider has received annual hands-on training on the administration of auto-injectors (Epi-Pen, Allerject) and emergency response procedures.
3. **Emergency Storage**: Auto-injectors must travel with the child during outdoor play and community outings and must never be kept in locked cupboards during child care hours (they must remain readily accessible to adults while out of reach of children).
